Sheet template: four tiers

Every sheet uses the same order. 1 · Identity: what this is, big. 2 · Do: the one or two actions, right under it. 3 · Details: editable properties in one grouped list with short left labels. 4 · Footer: who and when, with Delete on the same quiet line. One yellow key per sheet, always in tier 2. Synthetic data.
