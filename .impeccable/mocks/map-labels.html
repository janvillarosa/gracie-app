Map: names and category pips instead of numbers

Synthetic data (Thu 15, Sintra). Every stop gets a category pip, and the name is drawn beside it when there's room. MapLibre's collision detection decides which names fit. The pips always show; the names drop away in priority order: the selected stop first, then the next stop, then the day's order.

Desktop · zoomed to the day

All names fit, so they all show. The done stop is muted and struck through. The next stop gets a yellow ring and its name sits in a paper pill. Small arrows on the route show the order now that there are no numbers. Ideas show as hollow pips, only while the Ideas drawer is open.

Desktop · crowded day (12 stops), zoomed out

Names that would overlap are hidden, and the pips stay. The next stop always keeps its name. Stops too close to separate merge into a "+3" pip, and clicking it zooms in until they split. Hovering any pip, or its row in the list, shows that name.

Phone · read-only view

On the phone, names are shortened to about 14 characters. Fewer show because the screen is narrower. The bottom card always gives the full name.
