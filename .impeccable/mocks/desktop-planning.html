A · Planning desk: Ideas | Days | Map

Three columns you see at once. Ideas sit permanently on the left (drag one onto a day). The days are a wide column, and every date shows, empty ones as drop slots. The map is on the right and only takes space once there are places. A "Ready to go?" checklist at the top replaces the Next stop card before the trip.

B · Day board: days as columns

The days lay out side by side as paper columns, like a weekly planner. Ideas are the first column, so dragging an idea right puts it on a day. You see the whole trip's shape at once (which days are packed, which are empty). The map is a toggle that slides in over the board for geography checks. For trips longer than about 5 days the board scrolls sideways, with the trip header and checklist fixed.
