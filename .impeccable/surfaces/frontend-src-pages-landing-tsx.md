## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the House's shared sheet. Every line is signed by whoever added or ticked it, and the visitor writes the next line. It refuses the category default: a split hero (copy left, phone mockup right) over a row of icon feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The Bauhouse app world. Ground #F7F7F7, white paper sheets with peeking edges, ink #1A1A1A, yellow key #FFD417 (the only accent on actions), red #BE1E2D and blue #2348A6 as whole sheet fields, category stamps, the highlighter swipe. Outfit for display, Figtree for text. Sheets are told apart by tone and shaped edges (zigzag slip, torn till receipt with a rubber stamp), never hairline boxes.

STORY: First, one shared list for the whole house. Second, everyone sees who did what. Third, lists and trips. Fourth, it works with no signal. Fifth, it's free. Then start a House.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A nav bar. A centred two-line H1, "Manage your [word]" over "together.", at about 92px. The word cycles through life, groceries, chores, trips, shopping and lists, with the highlighter swiping under each new one (asked for by Jan; on phones the word takes its own middle line). Under it, a large white "Why Bauhouse" sheet (about 820px) with a peeking edge, its four pitch rows signed by Mia, Sam and Jo, and the live add bar at the bottom. The yellow "Start your House, free" key sits beside the add bar. The sheet runs off the bottom edge.

FORM: Grounded structure 6 of 7, "the page is a list you write on" (dealt lead). tasteskill direction page-is-the-object. Signature interaction: the live add bar lands the visitor's item with the highlighter and a stamp, then demo Sam ticks a line.
